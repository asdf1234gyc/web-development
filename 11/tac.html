Terms and Conditions

Ordering

The Lessee is responsible for ensuring the completeness and accuracy of all details prior to signing the rental form. Equipment shall not be deemed reserved until this Agreement is signed and payment is received via one or more of the following methods, at the Lessor's discretion: payment through the portal, completion of a full credit card authorization or ACH form, or receipt of a deposit check. Orders must be finalized 14 days prior to the delivery date, including equipment quantities, details, and final payment. Any changes thereafter are subject to availability and will incur additional fees, including but not limited to cancellation fees.

Damage Waiver

All rental orders require payment of an 8% non-refundable damage waiver fee. The Lessee understands that this waiver is not insurance. The damage waiver covers normal wear and tear on rented equipment. Excessive damage or negligent maintenance beyond normal wear and tear is not covered by the damage waiver fee, and the Lessee will be required to pay additional fees.

Payment

Rental fees must be paid in advance. Valid credit card and/or ACH bank information must be provided as a guarantee for replacement costs of lost, damaged, or stolen items. Booking orders require a 50% deposit and the signing of this Agreement. The balance is due 14 days prior to items leaving the warehouse. The Lessor accepts only Visa, MC, American Express, company checks, and ACH, and these policies may change at any time. A 3% processing fee will be added to all credit card payments. To avoid the processing fee, the Lessee may pay by check or ACH. Fees for all lost or damaged items are due upon receipt and will be deducted from the on-file credit card and/or ACH. Upon receipt of the deposit with an electronic signature on the contract, the Lessor deems the contract and terms accepted.

Cancellation and Rescheduling

Orders must be canceled at least 90 days prior to the delivery date to receive a full deposit refund. Orders canceled 31 to 89 days prior to the delivery date will incur a 50% charge of the invoice amount. Orders canceled within 30 days prior to the delivery date will incur the full rental fee. Full payment is due 14 days prior to the delivery date. Additional orders will be accepted if applicable. The Lessor permits the Lessee to reschedule events within 18 months after the original booking date. Extensions beyond 18 months from the original booking date require the Lessor's written approval. All rescheduled bookings require payment of a 50% cancellation fee and must pay the full balance within 14 days after the rescheduled event date. Pandemics, inclement weather, and/or other unforeseen issues do not alter the terms of the cancellation policy. However, if the Lessee's event occurs in an area subject to a mandatory closure or evacuation order by state or federal authorities, a refund or credit will be provided to the Lessee.
