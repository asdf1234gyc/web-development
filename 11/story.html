About Karoomba!

Karoomba! is a professional theme party and event planning company dedicated to creating unique and memorable celebration experiences for our clients.

Founded in 2010, we specialize in transforming ordinary spaces into extraordinary party venues with our creative themes, professional event planning, and attention to detail.

Our History

Since our establishment in 2010, we have successfully planned over 1,000 events, ranging from intimate birthday parties to large-scale corporate functions.

Our journey began with a small team of passionate event planners in Shanghai, and we've since expanded to serve clients across China with our signature blend of creativity and professionalism.

Our Team

We pride ourselves on having a diverse team of creative and passionate professionals, including:

  • Certified Event Planners with 5+ years experience
  • Talented Theme Designers specializing in decor and ambiance
  • Technical Specialists for lighting, sound and special effects
  • Dedicated Customer Service Representatives

Our team members come from various creative backgrounds including theater, design, and hospitality, bringing unique perspectives to every event.

Our Core Values

  • Creativity & Innovation - We constantly push boundaries to deliver fresh, exciting concepts
  • Client-Centric Approach - Your vision is our priority from planning to execution
  • Attention to Detail - We sweat the small stuff so you don't have to
  • Collaborative Spirit - Teamwork makes every event better
  • Professional Integrity - Honesty and transparency in all our dealings
  • Continuous Improvement - Always learning and evolving our craft

These values guide every decision we make and every event we create.
