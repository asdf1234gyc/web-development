Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book my event?

We recommend booking at least 3-6 months in advance for most events, and 6-12 months for large events or weddings to ensure availability of your preferred date and adequate planning time.

What is your pricing structure?

Our pricing varies based on event size, complexity, and services required. After your initial consultation, we'll provide a detailed quote tailored to your specific needs.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Absolutely! We work with caterers who can accommodate various dietary needs including vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and allergy-specific requirements.

Do you handle venue booking?

Yes, we can assist with venue selection and booking as part of our comprehensive planning services, or work with a venue you've already selected.
